## The Biometric Triad

Every morning, cross-reference 3 raw telemetry markers against your 30-day baseline:
1. **Heart Rate Variability (HRV rMSSD in ms):** Proxy for autonomic parasympathetic tone.
2. **Resting Heart Rate (RHR in bpm):** Lower than baseline indicates cardiovascular recovery.
3. **Respiratory Rate (breaths/min):** Elevated rate (> +1.0 bpm) signals acute immune response or dehydration.

| Recovery State | Biometric Telemetry | Training Adjustment | Cognitive / Work Adjustment |
