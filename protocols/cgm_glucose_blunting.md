# Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) & Glucose Spike Blunting Matrix

> **Core Axiom:** Postprandial glucose spikes above +30 mg/dL trigger reactive hypoglycemia, cognitive fatigue, and sympathetic anxiety cascades.

---

## The 4-Step Meal Architecture

Apply this behavioral eating sequence to flatten your glycemic response curve by up to 40%:

1. **Step 1: The Soluble Fiber Cushion (Greens & Prebiotics)**
   * Ingest leafy greens, broccoli, or chia/psyllium husk 10 minutes prior to starches.
   * Creates a viscous mesh in the upper intestine, slowing carbohydrate absorption.
2. **Step 2: Protein & Healthy Lipids**
   * Slows gastric emptying via peptide YY (PYY) and cholecystokinin (CCK) release.
3. **Step 3: Complex Carbohydrates / Starches**
   * Keep glycemic load buffered behind fiber and protein.
4. **Step 4: The 10-Minute Post-Meal Soleus Walk**
   * Walk briskly or perform seated soleus pushups within 20 minutes of finishing the meal.
   * Stimulates skeletal muscle **GLUT4 translocation**, clearing blood glucose without demanding pancreatic insulin secretion.

---

## Biometric Sync & Telemetry Targets

* **Peak Excursion Delta:** Keep post-meal rise $< +30\text{ mg/dL}$ above baseline.
* **Return-to-Baseline Runway:** Return to fasting baseline within 120 minutes.
* **Overnight Glycemic Stability:** Standard deviation $< 15\text{ mg/dL}$ during sleep.
