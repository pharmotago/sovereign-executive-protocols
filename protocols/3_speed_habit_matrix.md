# The 3-Speed Habit Matrix (Floor, Target, Stretch)

> **Core Axiom:** You do not rise to the level of your goals; you fall to the level of your Floor Gear.

---

## The 3 Operating Gears

Every habit you install must be defined across 3 distinct neurological speeds:

| Gear | Time Commitment | Target Energy | Purpose | Example: Physical Training | Example: Writing / Engineering |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🌱 Floor Gear** | 2–5 Minutes | 10% (Exhausted, Sick, Travel) | Preserve Identity Streak | 10 bodyweight squats or 120s stretch | Write 1 line of code / 1 sentence |
| **⚡ Target Gear** | 15–20 Minutes | 60–80% (Normal Working Day) | Progressive Competence | 20m Zone 2 cardio or dumbbell circuit | 1 uninterrupted Pomodoro block |
| **🚀 Stretch Gear** | 45–60 Minutes | 100% (High HRV, Green Day) | Exponential Mastery | Heavy lifting + sprint finish | 2 hours deep flow state output |

---

## The Never-Zero-Twice Protocol

1. **Rule 1:** Missing 1 day is human variance. Missing 2 consecutive days is the birth of an avoidance loop.
2. **Rule 2:** The morning after an interrupted streak, drop immediately into **Floor Gear**. Do not attempt to "make up" missed volume with an exhausting marathon.
3. **Rule 3:** A 2-minute Floor Gear action counts as a 100% valid identity vote.
